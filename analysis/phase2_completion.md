## Blockers and evidence gaps

No blocker prevents completion of the Phase 2 evidence model. The following limitations constrain later claims:

- **delivery_and_depth:** Slides and tasks establish artifact content, not actual classroom delivery, duration or intended mastery. Depth is an explicitly labeled inference. Recordings were indexed but not watched or transcribed. No timing estimates or claims that a listed topic was delivered live.
- **assessment_scope:** Assessment mappings use reviewed task statements and selected baseline exams, not an exhaustive question-by-question audit of every historical exam. Starter code, hidden tests and student submissions were not executed or inspected comprehensively. Observed means the task is present; unknown does not mean unassessed. No workload or implementation-success claim.
- **local_baseline_dates:** The supplied local files form one composite baseline. Deck order and older exam/proposal dates are artifact provenance, not a reconstructed single-semester calendar. Content can support comparison; exact prior pacing and actual delivery remain unknown.
- **local_optional_material:** Some slide sections explicitly say skipped, further reading or not in exam. These qualifiers and assessment exclusions are retained. HW1A supplied prediction code is not counted as a required prediction implementation. Reading-only/brief evidence must not be promoted to developed teaching or assessed implementation.
- **local_deck_assessments_missing:** The configured local slide collections do not supply a complete assessment/project inventory. Abbeel has image-based algorithm slides; selected DQN images were visually inspected. Teaching mappings are usable; assessment/project unknowns remain.
- **cs234_variants:** Pre-class and post-class decks differ in scope; mapping favors the inspected post-class sequence. HW3 has a 2025 header despite its 2026 location. Phase 1 also records an inaccessible FAQ. Lecture labels are approximate order, not separate offerings or teaching counts. HW3 is identified by its actual artifact, with year ambiguity retained.
- **cs224r_supplementary_and_ipo:** Supplementary PowerPoint content and recordings were not fully reviewed. A linked optional paper uses IPO for a different title/method from the default project’s Azar-style squared identity objective. The project objective is normalized separately; the optional IPO paper is left unmapped. Numbered PDF lecture filenames skip 14.
- **optional_readings:** Catalog titles/sections establish reading associations and optional status where explicit; linked papers are not all full-text reviewed. General textbook recommendations remain at source level. A reading pointer establishes neither taught depth nor complete paper contents.
- **book_mapping_scope:** Mappings cover the normalized vocabulary using the configured edition and precise section/page references; this is not a complete reverse index of every book subtopic. Many modern named methods have no verified direct section match. Related background is explicitly distinguished. An unmapped method is not a proved absence from the book, and this phase makes no judgment about important omissions from the proposed curriculum.
- **negative_evidence:** The review does not support exhaustive absence claims across every modality and resource. Unestablished source-topic pairs are unknown rather than not-found. Matrix blanks cannot be used as evidence that a course does not teach a topic.
