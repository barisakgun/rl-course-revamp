## Accepted scope and classifications

### Reinforcement learning formulation / Markov decision processes / State and feature representation and related concepts

Group: `formulation`.

**core** (accepted); accepted mastery: explain, analyze; status: foundational; delivery: live.

Accepted scope: Formulate finite episodic/discounted MDPs; distinguish observed information from a Markov state. No POMDP solver or abstraction theorem. Stochastic policy parameterization revisited in policy gradients.

Topic IDs: `rl_formulation`, `mdp`, `state_representation`, `partial_observability`, `reward_return`, `value_functions`, `policy_parameterization`, `environment_models`.

Assessment path to review later: exam, project.

### Bellman expectation equations / Bellman optimality equations / Backups and Bellman operators and related concepts

Group: `planning`.

**core** (accepted); accepted mastery: derive, implement, analyze; status: foundational, classical; delivery: live.

Accepted scope: Derive finite-MDP Bellman backups and improvement argument; implement one VI/PI solver, compare the other. No full convergence-proof sequence. GPI reused in control.

Topic IDs: `bellman_expectation`, `bellman_optimality`, `bellman_operators`, `dynamic_programming`, `policy_evaluation`, `policy_improvement`, `policy_iteration`, `value_iteration`, `generalized_policy_iteration`.

Assessment path to review later: exam, assignment.

### On-policy and off-policy learning / Online versus offline data access / Monte Carlo prediction and related concepts

Topic IDs: `on_off_policy_learning`, `online_offline_learning`, `monte_carlo_prediction`, `sampling_bootstrapping`, `temporal_difference_learning`, `bias_variance`, `n_step_returns`.

Assessment path to review later: exam, assignment.
