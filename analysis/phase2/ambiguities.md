## forward_returns_vs_traces

## Evidence gaps

### delivery_and_depth

Sources: silver_rl, berkeley_cs285, stanford_cs234, stanford_cs224r, current_course, foundations-deep-rl-abbeel, sutton.

Slides and tasks establish artifact content, not actual classroom delivery, duration or intended mastery. Depth is an explicitly labeled inference. Recordings were indexed but not watched or transcribed.

**Effect:** No timing estimates or claims that a listed topic was delivered live.

**Possible follow-up:** Inspect recordings only if a later specific decision depends on delivery or duration.

### assessment_scope

Sources: silver_rl, berkeley_cs285, stanford_cs234, stanford_cs224r, current_course, foundations-deep-rl-abbeel, sutton.

Assessment mappings use reviewed task statements and selected baseline exams, not an exhaustive question-by-question audit of every historical exam. Starter code, hidden tests and student submissions were not executed or inspected comprehensively.

**Effect:** Observed means the task is present; unknown does not mean unassessed. No workload or implementation-success claim.

**Possible follow-up:** Audit relevant task/code details during assessment design if requested.

### local_baseline_dates

Sources: current_course.

The supplied local files form one composite baseline. Deck order and older exam/proposal dates are artifact provenance, not a reconstructed single-semester calendar.

**Effect:** Content can support comparison; exact prior pacing and actual delivery remain unknown.

**Possible follow-up:** Use the composite baseline as authorized; ask about pacing only when needed.

### local_optional_material

Sources: current_course.

Some slide sections explicitly say skipped, further reading or not in exam. These qualifiers and assessment exclusions are retained. HW1A supplied prediction code is not counted as a required prediction implementation.

**Effect:** Reading-only/brief evidence must not be promoted to developed teaching or assessed implementation.

**Possible follow-up:** Consult the cited qualifiers when evaluating baseline depth.

### local_deck_assessments_missing

**Effect:** An unmapped method is not a proved absence from the book, and this phase makes no judgment about important omissions from the proposed curriculum.

**Possible follow-up:** Use these references for a separately requested book-grounded gap analysis; inspect additional sections as needed.
