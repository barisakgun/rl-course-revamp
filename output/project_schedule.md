# Project schedule

Generated by `python3 scripts/phase6_report.py` from accepted decisions and explicit analysis assumptions. Calendar weeks are assessment targets; week.slot labels identify logical teaching sessions. Actual-date checks remain deferred until after Phase 7.

| Milestone | Calendar target | Course grade | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal | Week 4 | 5% | 2 pages |
| Formulation/design | Week 7 (later in the week) | 5% | 2–3 pages |
| Progress and evaluation plan | Week 11 (early in the week) | 7.5% | 3–4 pages |
| Presentation | Week 14 | 7.5% | 10 minutes + 3 minutes discussion per team |
| Final report and reproducibility package | Week 16; One week before letter grades are due | 10% | 6–8 pages + references |

Teams of 2–3 encouraged; individual/four-person teams considered case by case.

Online, TA-led and recorded; instructor unavailable for live attendance. Each team attends only its own slot. TA and attending groups grade presentations; weight split, rubric, aggregation and moderation to be finalized.

3 tentative 120-minute slots, up to 8 teams each. 104 minutes of presentations/discussion leave 16 for transitions per slot. TA staffing and final team slots remain unconfirmed; do not assume the instructor can substitute in January.

Offline or LLM RL projects need early self-study, a runnable simple baseline and a compute-feasible fallback by Week 7. Do not require all teams to use late topics; advanced extensions cannot postpone the Week 11 progress evidence. Prefer small models, fixed datasets or supplied traces where appropriate, not costly model pretraining.

Final report dates and grading turnaround will be checked after Phase 7 against the official letter-grade deadline.
