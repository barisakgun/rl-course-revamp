## Slide 11 — Semester project

**On slide**

Formulate an RL problem, build a solution, compare with a meaningful baseline and evaluate the results.

| Milestone | Course grade |
| --- | ---: |
| Proposal | 5% |
| Formulation and design | 5% |
| Progress and evaluation | 7.5% |
| Presentation | 7.5% |
| Final report | 10% |

Teams of 2–3 encouraged. Individual and four-person teams considered case by case.

**Speaker notes — 2:00**

Project expectations scale with team size. A well-supported negative result can succeed. Novelty and benchmark superiority are not required. Communicate limitations as well as results. Further project details will follow during the semester.
